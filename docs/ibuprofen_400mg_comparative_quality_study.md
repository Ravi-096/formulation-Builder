## 1. Study Overview & Pharmacopeial Framework

This empirical investigation evaluated the comparative quality, physico-chemical equivalence, and in-vitro performance of **five commercially available film-coated Ibuprofen 400 mg tablet brands** (coded **A, B, C, D, and E**) purchased from retail pharmacy outlets in Sana’a, Yemen.

Quality control parameters were evaluated against compendial standards from the **United States Pharmacopeia (USP)** and the **British Pharmacopoeia (BP)**.

## 4. Key Scientific Insights & Formulation Takeaways

1. **Excipient Mass Discrepancies**:
   - Total tablet weights varied by **66%**, from **557.7 mg (Brand A)** to **926.0 mg (Brand D)** for the exact same active dose (400 mg Ibuprofen).
   - This indicates substantially different excipient loading (diluents/binders such as Microcrystalline Cellulose vs. Dibasic Calcium Phosphate) across manufacturers.

2. **Decoupling of Hardness and Disintegration**:
   - Compaction pressure strongly influenced hardness, ranging from **6.73 kg/cm²** to **17.52 kg/cm²**.
   - Crucially, tablet crushing strength was not directly proportional to disintegration time. Brand E exhibited the highest hardness (**17.52 kg/cm²**) yet disintegrated rapidly in **2.40 min**. This demonstrates that superdisintegrant selection (e.g. Croscarmellose Sodium or Crospovidone via capillary action and swelling) dominates disintegration kinetics over tablet compaction density.

3. **In-Vitro Dissolution Rate**:
   - USP standards mandate **≥ 80% (Q) dissolved within 60 minutes** in 900 mL 0.1 N HCl (Apparatus II Paddle at 50 rpm).
   - All five brands achieved &gt; 80% dissolution within **30 minutes**, confirming prompt active drug release at gastric pH.

4. **Compendial Compliance Summary**:
   - **Weight Uniformity:** 100% compliance with BP limits (deviation &lt; ±5%).
   - **Friability:** 100% compliance with BP/USP limits (&lt; 1.0% loss).
   - **Disintegration:** 100% compliance with film-coated tablet specifications (&lt; 30 min).
   - **HPLC Assay:** 100% compliance with USP (90–110%); Brands A, D, and E also satisfied BP (95–105%).
   - **Hardness:** Only Brand D fell within the BP recommended crushing range (5–8 kg/cm²), while Brands A, B, C, and E exceeded 8 kg/cm² (up to 17.52 kg/cm²). However, hardness is recognized as an in-process, non-compendial physical parameter.
